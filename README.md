# Clean Plate Club

A local-first daily task tracker with a dynamic Eisenhower matrix. This is a testing build, feedback is welcome.

## Getting started

1. Open the app here: **https://adrellordaor.github.io/Clean-Plate-Club/**
2. Use **Microsoft Edge**. Other Chromium browsers should work, but Opera GX has a known crash bug with this app, avoid it for now.
3. On first load, the app will ask you to pick a folder on your computer to save your data. Choose any folder you're comfortable with, this is where your tasks live. If your browser doesn't support that prompt, the app falls back to saving inside the browser itself, no folder needed, but your data then only exists in that one browser on that one device.

## What it does

- **Daily Plate / Fridge**: your active tasks vs. everything else waiting in the background.
- **Eisenhower matrix**: tasks are scored on importance and urgency and placed into quadrants automatically, not manually sorted.
- **Habits**: recurring daily, weekly, or monthly items, tracked separately from one-off tasks.
- **Subtasks**: break a task down further; subtasks inherit their parent's priority rather than being scored on their own.

## Your data is yours alone

Nothing syncs between people using this link. Each person's tasks are saved only to the folder (or browser) they pick on their own device.

## This is a testing build

Things may break or change. If something looks wrong or confusing, note what you were doing when it happened, that's the most useful feedback.
