Settings

Urgency is recalculated every day from these thresholds. Changing one re-scores every task straight away.

Deadline urgency (days before the deadline)

The score climbs between these points and hits 100 on the day or when overdue.

Staleness urgency (days since last touched, for tasks with no deadline)

Editing, checking off or bumping a task resets its clock.

Priority score

priority = weight × importance (Low 25 · Medium 50 · High 75 · Critical 100) + (1 − weight) × urgency. 0.5 weighs both equally; the score sets how vivid each row's colour is.

Overview

The split is the boundary between low and high on both axes (62.5 sits between Medium 50 and High 75), so it decides which quadrant a task belongs to everywhere, including the Calendar's Red rule and gold glow. The panel lists the top N tasks plus anything at or above the flag score; the Calendar reuses that same rule for its accented upcoming-deadline marker.

Staleness check-ins (Daily Digest, undated tasks only)

Once an undated task's days-untouched count crosses a new multiple of the re-flag interval, it gets a low-key check-in ("Just so you know: untouched for 21 days") in its own card under the Priority Today panel, colored in shades of teal — the Plan quadrant's hue — by whichever of the three day thresholds it's currently past. Separate from the staleness thresholds above, which drive urgency scoring, not this reminder.

Now window

A task planned for today scores at least this much urgency. Keep it above the quadrant split so being in Now genuinely moves a task into Do (important) or Clear (less important), instead of just looking more urgent while it stays in Plan or Backlog. Never lowers a score that a real deadline or staleness already put higher.

List view

Windows is the everyday Now/Later layout; Full is the complete folder-organized page with category tabs and the habit boxes. Daily habits always appear in Now; a weekly habit appears on its own weekday and once the (Monday-start) week is this many days from ending — 3 means from Thursday on; a monthly habit likewise on its day-of-month or this many days before the month ends. Weekly and monthly habits not due soon sit in Later instead. Habits never join the scoring.

Calendar

Pace is the existing red/green/blue/gray coloring. Capacity instead shades each day by effort ÷ this many points: a regular task is worth 1 point as Bite-size or 2 as a Main Course, a recurring habit completion/occurrence is a flat 1. Past days count what actually got done (completed_at); future days count what's scheduled (do_date). A distinct marker appears once a day crosses 100%.
