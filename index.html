    Settings

    Urgency is recalculated every day from these thresholds. Changing one re-scores every task straight away.

    Deadline urgency (days before the deadline)

    The score climbs between these points and hits 100 on the day or when overdue.

    Staleness urgency (days since last touched, for tasks with no deadline)

    Editing, checking off or bumping a task resets its clock.

    Priority score

    priority = weight × importance (Low 25 · Medium 50 · High 75 · Critical 100) + (1 − weight) × urgency. 0.5 weighs both equally; the score sets how vivid each row's colour is.

    Overview

    The split is the low/high boundary on both axes; the panel lists the top N tasks plus anything at or above the flag score.

    Staleness check-ins (Daily Digest, undated tasks only)

    Re-flags an undated task every N days untouched, colored by which threshold it's past.

    Daily Plate window

    A task planned for today scores at least this much urgency. Keep it above the quadrant split so being in Daily Plate genuinely moves a task into Do (important) or Clear (less important), instead of just looking more urgent while it stays in Plan or Backlog. Never lowers a score that a real deadline or staleness already put higher.

    List view

    A weekly habit also appears this many days before week's end (3 = from Thursday on); monthly works the same way. Not-due-soon habits sit in Fridge instead.

    Calendar

    Capacity view shades each day by effort ÷ this many points (Bite-size = 1 point, otherwise 2, a habit completion = 1); a marker appears past 100%.
